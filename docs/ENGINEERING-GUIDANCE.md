# Engineering guidance

BML installs project-local guidance from [jasonmcalpin/codex-engineering-guidelines](https://github.com/jasonmcalpin/codex-engineering-guidelines).

The reusable skills live under `.agents/skills/`:
- requirements-review
- architecture-review
- regression-fix
- change-verification
- refactoring

The source design, examples, installation notes, requirements-review cases, and attribution are retained under `docs/engineering/`. BML-specific instructions in the root `AGENTS.md` take precedence where more specific.
